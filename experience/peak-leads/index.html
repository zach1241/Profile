03 / SELECTED LESSONS

Delivery is more
than a launch.

Working across websites, ad accounts and search visibility made the relationship between implementation, measurement and client expectations impossible to ignore.

Read the Logic LC case study ↗
THREE TAKEAWAYS
  1. 01
    Protect search continuity

    A brand and website migration needs careful planning, URL mapping and monitoring. The ARR to True Line transition reinforced that lesson.

  2. 02
    Know what a conversion means

    Ad clicks, GA4 events and actual leads are not interchangeable. Tracking must reflect meaningful business actions.

  3. 03
    Distinguish delivery from launch

    ProCom's redesign is documented as work awaiting approval, not a published client website.
