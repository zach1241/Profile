In Trade CRM

Your services. Your bookings. All in one place.

The project

A booking-focused SaaS project for trade businesses, built around branded service pages, customer requests and a simpler business booking inbox.

Independent product design and full-stack development
SaaS · Booking flows · Authentication · Product UX · Responsive development
Active development
01 / The challenge

What needed to be solved

Smaller trade businesses need a straightforward way to explain services, gather structured customer details and keep track of incoming booking inquiries, without expensive operational overhead.

02 / Implementation

My development process

01 — Product scope

Defined an initial booking-first product experience around service setup, customer-facing booking pages and structured requests.

02 — Interface and onboarding

Designed the responsive landing page, visual identity, registration flows and clear navigation for trade businesses.

03 — Booking journey

Worked on staged customer questions, service choices, appointment requests and the corresponding business-side request workflow.

04 — Iterative development

Built and refined the application architecture while keeping external messaging, file-storage costs and payment functionality outside the current release scope.

03 / Outcome

What I delivered

An independently developed SaaS project in active development. The interface and booking journeys continue to evolve; this is not presented as a completed commercial release.
