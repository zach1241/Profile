01 / Context

The brief

A design or early idea is only the starting point. The challenge in this type of work is to turn the intended appearance and content structure into something that actually operates as a website.

02 / Work & approach

How I approach the project

01 — Translate the reference into a website

I begin by understanding the page structure, hierarchy, typography and components in the available design or concept. The implementation then needs to maintain those decisions across screen sizes.

02 — Build with WordPress and Elementor

I use WordPress and Elementor to assemble the experience into usable, editable sections. That includes thinking about content changes, navigation and layout behaviour rather than building solely for a screenshot.

03 — Review the real experience

The browser is the final test: spacing, responsiveness, interaction and consistency need to hold together when the page is actually being used. The linked walkthrough demonstrates the work in context.

03 / Reflection

What matters most

The important skill is not copying a layout pixel for pixel; it is translating a visual direction into a practical, maintainable website.
