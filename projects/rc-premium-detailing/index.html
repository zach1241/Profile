RC Premium Detailing

A custom Build Your Detail experience powered by WooCommerce.

The project

Designed and developed a tailored vehicle detailing configurator that lets customers choose their vehicle, services and extras rather than rely solely on fixed packages.

Custom WordPress and WooCommerce plugin developer
WordPress · Custom PHP plugin · WooCommerce · ACF Pro · JavaScript · Responsive UI
rcdetailing.co.za ↗
01 / The challenge

What needed to be solved

Detailing services can depend on vehicle category and customer preferences. A conventional WooCommerce catalog alone was not suited to the step-by-step bespoke service journey.

02 / Implementation

My development process

01 — Vehicle selection

Implemented a visual vehicle-category selection interface to begin the personalized detailing journey.

02 — Services and add-ons

Built configurable service discovery, selection and additional treatments within a custom multi-stage user experience.

03 — WooCommerce integration

Connected the custom WordPress plugin to WooCommerce product structures and checkout compatibility, including product mapping and pricing-related troubleshooting.

04 — Responsive refinement

Improved layout behavior for smaller screens and resolved plugin dependencies and editor-versus-live frontend issues during development.

03 / Outcome

What I delivered

A custom, WooCommerce-compatible vehicle service configurator. Payment completion and final checkout readiness should be validated before presenting the flow as fully launched.
