Logic LC

Structuring a technical-services website around what customers actually need to find.

01 / The problem

What needed to change

A technology integrator offers services that vary by geography, industry and technical requirements. Visitors need to find the right solution without learning the company's entire internal service taxonomy first.

02 / The approach

How I worked through it

Organizing information architecture

I worked on site structure that separates core service offerings from the industries they serve, making both navigation and search intent easier to understand.

Bringing clarity to complex services

Service and industry pages give the business room to explain security, networking and related solutions in an organized way, supported by suitable calls to action.

Iterating on the experience

The work involved WordPress/Elementor presentation, responsive adjustments, site navigation and supporting SEO content.

03 / Looking back

What I learned

When a service is complex, the website should simplify the buyer's decision rather than imitate the complexity of the business.
