logiclc.com ↗
01 / The challenge

What needed to be solved

Logic LC needed an effective way to present a broad range of technical services while keeping the site clear to navigate, professional and approachable for prospective business customers.

02 / Implementation

My development process

01 — Strategy and concepts

Created initial design directions, planned the site architecture and mapped the sitemap. Worked through concept and framework approvals before starting the build.

02 — Development

Developed the website in WordPress using Elementor, translating the approved visual direction into responsive pages, service information, clear navigation and conversion-focused calls to action.

03 — SEO and publishing

Structured headings and page content for relevant search intent, checked key on-page SEO elements, tested responsive presentation and completed the publishing workflow.

04 — Client editing experience

Named Elementor containers and organized the page structure to make routine content changes easier for the team without needing to reconstruct the layouts.

03 / Outcome

What I delivered

Published a full website with an organized editing experience and SEO-conscious page structure.
