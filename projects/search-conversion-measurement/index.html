Search & Conversion Measurement

Looking beyond traffic charts to the events that actually represent a lead.

01 / The problem

What needed to change

A site may generate visits and plenty of event counts while still leaving the business unsure how many people made a useful enquiry. A phone-click event, form-start and successful form submission are not interchangeable.

02 / The approach

How I worked through it

Defining what to measure

I separate visibility metrics from actions that could indicate commercial intent. Search Console helps explain organic discovery; GA4 and advertising platforms help investigate journeys when configured correctly.

Checking implementation

The practical work covers click tracking, form event testing, duplicate triggers and mobile conversion paths. The difference between a sent event and a verified enquiry is documented rather than assumed.

Connecting measurement to page changes

Analytics is most useful when it suggests a concrete page improvement: clearer phone access, functioning forms or a page that better matches the searcher's need.

03 / Looking back

What I learned

I would rather have a small set of trustworthy conversion signals than a dashboard that looks impressive but cannot answer a client question.
