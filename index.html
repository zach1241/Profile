06 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good to know before we start.

Every engagement starts with a conversation about what you're trying to achieve.

What's included in each package?+

Choose a category above and open “View all inclusions” on each plan for its specific monthly or project deliverables. We confirm the final scope in writing.

Is advertising spend included?+

No. Monthly advertising management fees are separate from the ad budget, which you pay directly to Google or Meta.

How do monthly retainers work?+

SEO and advertising plans are billed monthly for the agreed work. Websites are quoted as one-time projects. Terms, milestones, and any extras are agreed before starting.

Are backlinks or rankings guaranteed?+

No. Editorial links depend on third-party decisions. We track outreach and earned placements separately, and cannot promise fixed rankings, traffic or leads.

Can we work through Upwork?+

Yes. After reviewing your project and agreeing on scope, we can arrange an Upwork contract. There is no automatic checkout or contract created through this site.

Can I request a custom combination?+

Absolutely. Tell us which services, locations, website pages or campaigns you need and we'll discuss a tailored proposal.
