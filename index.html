A little about me

I like building things that solve real problems.

I'm Zachary, a developer based in Cape Town, South Africa. My work has grown from WordPress and website development into full-stack development, technical SEO and custom internal systems.

I enjoy taking something that is unclear, inefficient or difficult to manage and turning it into something structured — whether that's a website, a technical workflow or software used by an entire team.

The goal isn't to use the biggest technology stack. It's to build the right thing, make it reliable, and make sure the people using it understand how it works.
